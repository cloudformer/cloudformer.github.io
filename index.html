Cloud services that cost nothing while nobody uses them.

AWS's "serverless" databases, caches and schedulers still bill every hour, and so do the apps you host. IdleFree rebuilds them on Lambda, S3 and DynamoDB. You pay per request, and nothing when it's quiet.
